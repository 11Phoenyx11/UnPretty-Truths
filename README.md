# 🌸 UnPretty Truths

**UnPretty Truths** is a soul-born, reality-rooted creative project that exists to name, heal, and rise from the hard things we often avoid. It’s not about perfection—it’s about power. The kind you reclaim when you stop pretending and start standing in your truth.

This is a space for code, content, affirmations, automation, and creative healing tools that reflect personal growth, emotional transparency, and spiritual rebirth.

---

## 💡 Vision

To create raw, unfiltered resources and digital tools that help people:
- Confront uncomfortable truths
- Practice emotional self-care
- Reclaim their voice
- Rise from emotional survival to intentional living

Whether it's affirmations, scripts, automation templates, or healing prompts, this project supports the belief that *truth is the first form of freedom.*

---

## 🔥 What's Inside

- `affirmations/` – Daily truths wrapped in power and softness
- `scripts/` – Code for automation, journaling tools, or digital wellness prompts
- `content/` – Visuals, Canva links, social captions, and story prompts
- `planner-pages/` – Weekly wellness spreads, coloring pages, and more

## 💼 Role Summary: Customer Operations & Lead Response Specialist

Verdant Customer Service is looking for a Customer Operations & Lead Response Specialist to help small businesses stop losing money from missed calls, slow replies, and inconsistent follow-up. This is an execution role where speed, accuracy, and consistency matter.

### Key Responsibilities

- Respond to inbound leads via phone, SMS, email, website chat, and social DMs using approved scripts and brand voice.
- Run same-day and multi-day follow-up sequences to increase booked appointments/jobs.
- Schedule appointments and confirm details; reduce no-shows with reminders and reschedule workflows.
- Send review requests after service completion and escalate issues using a service-recovery script.
- Update CRM/spreadsheet trackers daily (lead source, status, next action, outcome).
- Produce a weekly performance summary (response time, leads contacted, booked rate, reviews gained).
- Follow documented SOPs and improve them over time (what’s working, what to tighten).
- Maintain confidentiality and handle customer information with care.

### Success Metrics (KPIs)

- Response time: under 5 minutes during coverage windows.
- Lead contact rate (same day): 80%+.
- Booking conversion rate increase month-over-month.
- No-show/cancellation reduction.
- Review volume + rating improvement.

### Required Qualifications

- 1–3+ years in customer service, call handling, lead intake, scheduling, or client success.
- Strong written communication (professional, warm, fast).
- Comfortable with CRMs and tools (Google Workspace, spreadsheets, SMS/email platforms).
- Reliable internet, quiet workspace, and ability to follow structured processes.
- Calm under pressure; consistent follow-through.

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Calendly, Podium, Zendesk, or similar.
- Experience in home services, healthcare, beauty/med spa, legal, or other appointment-based industries.
- Familiarity with review platforms (Google Business Profile, Yelp) and service recovery.

### Compensation

- Hourly or per-client retainer (with performance bonus tied to bookings/reviews).
